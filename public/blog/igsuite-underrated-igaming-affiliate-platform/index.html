Why iGsuite Is Still an Underrated iGaming Affiliate Platform

September 9, 2026 · John Wright

I keep running into the same pattern with affiliate program software. Everybody knows the big names. A lot of operators still default to whatever feels “safe.” And then you talk to people actually migrating right now, and iGsuite keeps showing up as one of the fastest growing platforms around.

My strong opinion: they’re still underrated. Not because the product is quiet. Because the category is sticky. Legacy platforms got the brand mindshare 10 or 15 years ago, and that inertia is real. But operators are moving. I’m hearing more migrations off some of the top established affiliate program software, and iGsuite is on that short list more often than the old conference chatter would suggest.

If you’re comparing platforms, start with our Affiliate Software Report (ASR) on NousViz. Then dig into the product itself at igsuite.com. And if you care about the analytics angle (you should), listen to my episode with Lazar Petrov from iGsuite.

The market got expensive. Reporting is not BI.

Lazar and I sat down on iGaming BI, Affiliate Analytics and the Cost of Bad Data. The through-line was simple. Back when traffic was cheap and everybody printed money, nobody needed real analytics. That world is gone.

“Back in the days… people didn’t care about data. You didn’t need data to know that you’re making money… Nowadays the cost is extremely high. The high quality traffic is very hard to find.”

Lazar Petrov, iGsuite

That’s the pain. Affiliate managers still get told to chase FTDs and affiliate count. Meanwhile the acquisition cost is climbing, PPC is a mess in a lot of geos, and if you can’t see cost and player value clearly, you’re guessing with margin.

“If you go to MyAffiliates, Income Access, NetRefer, all these platforms… they have just reporting. And this is not a BI.”

Lazar Petrov

Look, that line matters. A lot of “analytics” in this industry is a dashboard somebody requested once, never opened again, and called a strategy. Lazar’s blunt about that too.

“If I tell you how many different reports I have requested that I looked only once in my life… this is a great example… how useless can be too much information.”

Lazar Petrov

Simply put: you need the right data, accessible fast, tied to decisions. Not 40 unused Tableau tabs.

Why iGsuite still feels underrated

On the show I asked around what percentage of operators actually push affiliate-platform data into BI. The number I kept hearing was something like plus or minus 10%. Lazar didn’t flinch. He basically said yes, that’s about right. Most teams still aren’t connecting the stack properly. A lot of “BI” never even touches the affiliate software.

That gap is exactly where a modern affiliate program platform should win. Our industry is still massively affiliate-dependent. Lazar put it around 90% plus for a lot of the businesses he sees. If your affiliate software can’t give you clean layers (player, source, country, brand, cost), you’re blind on the channel that funds the company.

“But if you don’t have the cost, you are blind. You don’t know anything.”

Lazar Petrov

So when I say iGsuite is underrated, I mean this: people still overweight the logo of the legacy suite, and underweight whether the platform helps affiliate managers actually understand performance. I’m watching operators migrate for that reason. Not for a prettier login screen.

Legacy software inertia vs new platforms

Lazar’s take on the old guard matched what I see at conferences. The mastodons were innovative 15 years ago. The codebases and migration politics are brutal now. Prioritizing hundreds of clients onto a rewrite is a nightmare. That creates room.

“It’s a great opportunity now for these new softwares and tools… new platforms going to bring the opportunity for the new technology to enter the market.”

Lazar Petrov

Yeah. Rising tide for the platforms that ship. iGsuite is one of the ones moving. Fastest growing doesn’t mean “biggest yet.” It means the trajectory is real, and the migration conversations are real.

Where the Affiliate Software Report fits

I’m not going to pretend one podcast episode replaces due diligence. If you’re an operator or an affiliate program team evaluating software, use a structured comparison.

Read the StatsDrone Affiliate Software Report. Cross-check it against a live demo at igsuite.com. Ask about BI, exports, APIs, player-level visibility, and how painful a migration actually is. And if you want the philosophy behind why analytics matters in this category, start with the Lazar episode.

Episode: iGaming BI, Affiliate Analytics and the Cost of Bad Data with Lazar Petrov · Guest: Lazar Petrov · Also: our iGsuite software page.

Bottom line

iGsuite is one of the faster growing iGaming affiliate program platforms I see in market right now. Still underrated relative to the old logos. Gaining operators who are done pretending a report grid equals business intelligence.

If you’re stuck on “we’ve always used X,” wait a minute. Test whether X still helps you see cost, player value, and affiliate performance in a market where traffic got expensive. That’s the conversation Lazar and I had. That’s the conversation more operators are having before they migrate.
