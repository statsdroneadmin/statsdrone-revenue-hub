Trevor Lasn on 0xInsider and Who Is Actually Winning in Prediction Markets

September 22, 2026 · John Wright

Prediction markets keep showing up in more of my conversations. Not as a novelty. As a real information layer. When people put money behind a call, you get a different signal than a poll or a pundit take.

I sat down for a short written Q&A with Trevor I. Lasn, who built 0xInsider. The product is aimed at a simple question that is hard to answer in real time: who is actually winning, and what are they trading?

What is 0xInsider?

0xInsider helps traders see who’s actually winning in prediction markets and what they’re trading in real time. We track smart money, whale activity, trader performance, and market flows across platforms like Polymarket and Kalshi.

Why did you create it?

I was spending hours digging through on chain data trying to figure out who consistently makes good predictions. I built 0xInsider because that information should be accessible in real time.

What interests you about prediction markets and where is the space headed?

Prediction markets are becoming a better source of information than news, polls, or pundits because participants have money on the line. I think we’ll see prediction markets expand far beyond politics into sports, finance, and everyday decision making.

Do the early days make it easier for advantage players to profit?

Yes. The market is still inefficient in many areas. As more liquidity, data, and professional traders enter the space, those edges will get smaller.

How will agents get involved?

Agents are a natural fit. They’ll monitor markets, analyze information, manage portfolios, and execute trades faster than humans. We’re already thinking about how to make 0xInsider useful for both human traders and agents.

Where to look next

If you want to see the product, start at 0xinsider.com. Prediction markets are moving fast. Tools that show who is winning (not just what the headline market price is) are going to matter more, not less.
