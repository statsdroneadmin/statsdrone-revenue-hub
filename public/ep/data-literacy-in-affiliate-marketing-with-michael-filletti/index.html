About This Episode

Meet Michael Filletti who's the co-founder of Routy, or rather Routy.app. Michael is passionate about Business Intelligence and applying BI and analytics to affiliate marketing. His comments on data literacy in iGaming and affiliate marketing are very much real and it is through tools like Routy which aim to unlock valuable insights that would make anybody a data analyst for optimizing their affiliate business. He sums up the state of the iGaming industry very well which is more people need to get comfortable with data, to analyze data and he believes it is these skills which can help people grow in their career and affiliates grow their revenue.
