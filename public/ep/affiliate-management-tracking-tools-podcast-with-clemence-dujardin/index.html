About This Episode

Meet Clemence Dujardin who is the group CEO of MyAffiliates. She is a thought leader when it comes to affiliate marketing as her company, MyAffiliates, is a fast growing and well established affiliate marketing software. Clemence shares her insights as to what she sees currently happening in affiliate marketing and in iGaming and we discuss the state of affiliate management today as more affiliate managers enter into this new career.

Her accolades include winning CEO Woman of the year 2021 by Sigma Awards and B2B excellence award - Women in iGaming Diversity.
