How Affiliate Programs Can Improve Their Results

📅 November 30, 2023 ⏱️ 10:29
▶ Play Episode View Details

About This Episode

What can affiliate programs do to earn more revenue and improve their business?

The old method was to get affiliate managers to find more affiliates, problem solved, right?

Not quite.

While most programs are trying to solve the problem with volume, there are 3 things affiliate programs should be doing instead.

1. Use data to make data driven decisions
2. Use CRO to build better landing pages that convert better
3. Train your affiliate managers with the right tools so they can do their job better.
