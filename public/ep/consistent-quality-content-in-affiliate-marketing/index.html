Consistent quality content in affiliate marketing

📅 December 24, 2024 ⏱️ 7:43
▶ Play Episode View Details

About This Episode

Today we have a solo cast from me talking about being consistent in affiliate marketing. There is a time to pivot like the number of betting affiliates I've seen switch to cryptocurrencies and a time to stay consistent.  You have to decide what that consistent looks like for you. For the most successful affiliates I have known, they all have something in common: consistency!

Yes, they are always producing content but it isn't just about volume but it comes down to both consistent and quality. 

I've reflected on what Steve Toth told me in a previous recording that he was advised to be consistent with his newsletter if he was going to start one. 5 years later, he has one of the best SEO newsletters around.
