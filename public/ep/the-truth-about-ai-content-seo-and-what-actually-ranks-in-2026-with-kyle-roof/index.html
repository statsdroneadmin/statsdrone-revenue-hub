The Truth About AI Content, SEO, and What Actually Ranks in 2026 with Kyle Roof

📅 April 30, 2026 ⏱️ 36:39
▶ Play Episode View Details

About This Episode

In this episode, John sits down with Kyle Roof, the mind behind Page Optimizer Pro, to unpack what’s really happening in SEO as AI reshapes the landscape. While many claim SEO is dying, Kyle argues the opposite: it’s evolving and those who understand the fundamentals are winning more than ever.

They dive deep into why AI-generated content often fails to rank, what Google is actually rewarding in 2026, and why “term richness” and contextual relevance still matter more than hype. Kyle shares insights from ongoing case studies analyzing AI content, revealing where it falls short and how to fix it.

The conversation also explores programmatic SEO, building content systems that scale, and how SaaS companies and affiliates can rethink their strategy to stay competitive. If you’ve been feeling lost in the noise around AI and SEO, this episode brings clarity and a practical roadmap forward.

You can reach Kyle on any of his social media channels (like here) and check out pageoptimizer.pro.
